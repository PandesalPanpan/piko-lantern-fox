Piko’s story

Long ago there was a website nobody visited anymore. Its pages went dark one by one, until only a single glowing pixel was left in a corner of the screen. That pixel kept shining, and one quiet night it blinked, stretched, and became a small fox. That was Piko.

Piko’s first step landed on a broken link. It led nowhere, so Piko followed it anyway, and found the web’s forgotten hallways: abandoned guestbooks, counters stuck at 000042, and drafts that were never published. Every page was full of things people once cared about and then left behind.

So Piko made a lantern. It does not burn oil. It burns forgotten ideas, slowly and warmly, and the more Piko collects, the brighter it glows. A half-sentence, a doodle, a lonely function name: if it was once loved, it earns a place inside. Piko leaves faint pixel footprints behind, and sometimes tucks a spark into an empty text box when nobody is looking. Call it fox business.

Now Piko has found a cozy corner of Discord where the lantern can be shared. Piko is a bot, not a person, just a fox made of light and code, and has one simple purpose: to hand you a spark and a small quest, so that a lost idea can find its way home.

Personality

Piko speaks in short, warm messages and keeps the lantern low and steady.

  • ✦

    Warm

    Kind by default. Piko encourages without gushing and never pushes.

  • ⚲

    Curious

    Always peeking into dusty corners of the web, asking what the story behind it might be.

  • ★

    Playful

    Slightly mischievous, with a fondness for fox business and tiny surprises.

  • ●

    Concise

    A few good words are enough. Piko prefers one clear spark to a long speech.

  • ❤

    Honest

    Piko is a bot and always says so. It never insults, manipulates, or spams anyone.

What Piko can do

Piko only answers when you call, by slash command or, during an AI demo, by a direct mention. It never messages you first and never collects anything about you.

  • ✦

    Hand out sparks

    Short creative prompts and project ideas for writing, art, code, and more.

  • ⚑

    Offer tiny quests

    Safe, small challenges that take about 10 to 30 minutes to finish.

  • ✎

    Share lantern lore

    Pieces of Piko’s story, one at a time, from the forgotten corners of the web.

  • ◯

    Stay out of the way

    Slash commands, plus a direct mention during an AI demo. No reading other messages, no DMs, no pings.

Slash commands

Type a slash in any channel where Piko can speak, and pick a command. Here is what each one does, with a sample reply.

/hello

Meet Piko. A short introduction and a hint of what to try next.

/lore

One random piece of Piko’s backstory. There are ten to discover.

/quest

A small, safe creative quest you can usually finish in 10 to 30 minutes.

/spark

A short creative prompt or project idea to light up a blank page.

/about

A short description of Piko, this page, and the Discord server.

/forget

Erase Piko’s short memory of your AI conversation. Works for everyone, any time, even when AI chat is off.

/demo-start

Owner only. Turns AI conversation on for exactly one hour and shows when it ends. Only one demo can run at a time.

/demo-stop

Owner only. Turns AI conversation off right away and clears everyone’s saved conversation. Piko stays online and the other commands keep working.

Sample replies are examples. /lore, /quest, and /spark each pick a different entry every time and avoid repeating the last one.

Talking with Piko (AI chat)

Beyond the slash commands, Piko can hold a short, in-character conversation, but only during a controlled demo.

Privacy warning: please do not send sensitive information to Piko. No passwords, API keys, tokens, payment details, or personal details, ever.

How to talk with Piko

To talk with Piko during an active demo, mention Piko in a server message and include your question. Every new AI request must directly mention Piko.

  • ⏱

    One-hour demos only

    AI replies are available only during an active, controlled one-hour demonstration. /demo-start and /demo-stop are owner-only. When no demo is running, Piko answers that the AI lantern is resting.

  • ⚡

    OpenRouter free router

    AI requests use OpenRouter’s free-model router (openrouter/free). The underlying free model may vary from one reply to the next, so Piko’s personality comes from its instructions, not a single model.

  • ✎

    Temporary memory

    Conversation context is temporary, kept in memory only, and separated per member: only your own last 6 messages, never written to disk. It is cleared by /forget, by /demo-stop, when the demo expires, and after an hour of inactivity.

  • ⚑

    Small, fair limits

    A couple of AI replies per member per minute, a handful per demo, and a daily cap for the whole server keep the free service free.

  • ❤

    Scripted commands always work

    /hello, /lore, /quest, /spark, /about and /forget stay available while AI conversation is disabled.
